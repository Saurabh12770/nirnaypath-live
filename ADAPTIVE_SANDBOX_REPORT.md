# Phase 11D: Adaptive Sandbox Report

## Experimental Sandbox
A hidden experimental adaptive testing engine (`AdaptiveSandboxService.js`) has been deployed. 

## Mechanism
*   **Shadow Calibration**: It calculates IRT-style streaks behind the scenes during live exams.
*   **Invisibility**: Candidates are completely unaware of the sandbox. It does not affect the actual sequence of questions they receive.
*   **No Scoring Impact**: Experimental difficulty recommendations are logged for research purposes but do not alter the official exam score.

## Conclusion
The sandbox is safely gathering large-scale calibration data required for future live adaptive exam rollouts.
