# Operations Training Framework

## Overview
Technology scales seamlessly, but operations fail when humans err. The Operations Training Framework mandates that no institutional user receives administrative access to NirnayPath without passing strict interactive simulations.

## Required Modules
1. **Incident Response (War Room):** Trainees are placed in a simulated "P0 Redis Failure" and must execute the failover protocol under a timer.
2. **Fraud Board Evaluation:** Trainees review 5 historical mock cases of cheating, identifying false positives versus actual anomalies.
3. **Disaster Recovery Controls:** Validating understanding of the `OperationsTelemetryService` and when it is legally safe to trigger `extendTimer` globally.
4. **Legal Compliance & Support:** Training on generating immutable audit exports and handling accessibility accommodation requests before exam lock.

**Outcome:** Only certified operators are provisioned `Bearer` tokens with `ADMIN_TIER_2` or higher privileges.
