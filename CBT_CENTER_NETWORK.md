# NATIONAL CBT CENTER ORCHESTRATION

## 1. Network Orchestration
Deploying a CBT on a national scale requires robust physical center management. NirnayPath maps candidates to centers intelligently, prioritizing load-balancing and geographic constraints.

## 2. Core Capabilities
- **Geo-Aware Distribution:** Candidates are matched to centers within optimal travel distances.
- **Seat Mapping & Shift Balancing:** Prevents physical overload and standardizes shift throughput.
- **Overload Protection:** Centers enforce strict capacity caps in real-time, integrating with the scheduling engine to overflow to secondary locations.

## 3. Emergency Operations
- **Emergency Reassignment:** If a center fails (network blackout, infrastructure collapse), candidates are automatically hot-swapped to designated fallback centers or subsequent shifts.
- **Center Risk Scoring:** Centers receive dynamic reputation scores based on historical anomaly rates (fraud density, connectivity drops). 
- **Center Blacklisting:** High-risk centers are algorithmically flagged for operational blacklisting, removing them from future allocations.
