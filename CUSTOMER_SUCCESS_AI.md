# AI CUSTOMER SUCCESS ENGINE ARCHITECTURE

## Objective
Proactively manage institutional and creator health on NirnayPath by predicting churn, identifying onboarding friction, and providing explainable, advisory-only insights.

## Core Capabilities
1. **Institution Health Scoring:** Weighs factors like API error rates, candidate completion percentages, and support ticket frequency to generate a 0-100 score.
2. **Churn Prediction:** Flags institutions whose usage drops by >40% MoM.
3. **Proactive Suggestions:** Recommends "How to use PWA mode" to institutions with high candidate drop-offs.
4. **Creator Growth Insights:** Recommends topics/tags based on marketplace demand to EdTech creators.

## Privacy Mandate
Analyzes aggregate metadata. Never reads PII or exam question content directly. All outputs must be advisory, not automated interventions.
