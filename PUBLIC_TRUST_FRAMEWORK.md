# PUBLIC TRUST & APPEALS SYSTEM

## 1. Transparency as a Feature
NirnayPath ensures that candidates have a verifiable, immutable pathway to challenge results, answering keys, or fraud tags.

## 2. Appeal Workflows
- **Answer-Key Challenges:** Candidates can challenge ambiguous questions post-exam. Challenges are batched and routed to Subject Matter Experts (SMEs).
- **Normalization Appeals:** Transparency into the shift normalization formula used for their specific score.
- **Fraud-Review Appeals:** If flagged by the AI, candidates can request human-in-the-loop review of their telemetry/webcam footage.

## 3. Review Governance
- **Reviewer Independence:** Reviewers are blinded to candidate PII (Name, Demographics) during the review process to prevent bias.
- **Immutable Timelines:** Every step (Submission, Assignment, Review, Verdict) is timestamped and immutable.
- **Explainable Verdicts:** Final decisions must include structured, explainable reasoning linked to the specific evidence, fully accessible by the candidate.
