# Ecosystem Health Framework

## Overview
The Ecosystem Health Engine continuously evaluates the overall quality, trust, and operational vitality of the NirnayPath platform. It generates composite scores to provide executive visibility into the platform's macro-level health.

## Core Metrics Tracked
- **Institution Health:** Adherence to exam protocols and payment reliability.
- **Educator Trust Quality:** Content accuracy and behavioral integrity of creators.
- **Candidate Engagement:** Completion rates and active participation.
- **Marketplace Quality:** Aggregate rating and dispute rate of published content.
- **Operational Reliability:** System uptime and latency SLA adherence.
- **Support Responsiveness:** Ticket resolution times.
- **Onboarding Success:** Funnel conversion rates for new institutions and educators.

## Generated Outputs
- `ecosystemHealthScore`: A composite metric (0-100) representing overall platform vitality.
- `institutionalRiskScore`: An indicator of potential tenant churn or compliance failure.
- `creatorTrustScore`: A global reputation index for marketplace contributors.
