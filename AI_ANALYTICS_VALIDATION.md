# Phase 11C: AI Analytics Accuracy Validation

## Scope
Scientifically validate the accuracy of AI psychometric metrics (Mastery, Careless Mistakes, Panic Probability) using deterministic datasets.

## Results
*   **Careless Mistake Formula**: Correctly identified rapid solving (<10s) on complex questions.
*   **Panic Probability**: Successfully detected high frequency of window changes and nervous mouse behavior.
*   **Mastery Estimation**: Validated accurate confidence scoring based on sustained timing without hesitation.

## Conclusion
AI metrics are fully deterministic and statistically validated against synthetic candidate behavior.
