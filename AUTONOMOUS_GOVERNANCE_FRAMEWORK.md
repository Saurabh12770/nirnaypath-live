# Autonomous Governance Framework

## Overview
The Governance Intelligence Engine monitors the administrative and operational health of the NirnayPath ecosystem. It identifies bottlenecks, policy violations, and operational anomalies, recommending interventions to maintain institutional integrity.

## Capabilities
- **Identify Governance Bottlenecks:** Detects slow approval workflows or overloaded administrators.
- **Detect Institutional Policy Drift:** Identifies institutions deviating from standard compliance configurations.
- **Monitor Abuse Escalation Trends:** Analyzes patterns in reported fraud or support tickets.
- **Recommend Governance Interventions:** Suggests targeted audits or policy updates.
- **Analyze Support Governance Quality:** Evaluates resolution times and CSAT scores.
- **Detect Operational Anomalies:** Flags unusual administrative behavior.

## Core Principles
- **Explainable Recommendations:** Every intervention suggestion includes a clear justification based on telemetry data.
- **Auditability:** All governance insights are securely logged for forensic review.
