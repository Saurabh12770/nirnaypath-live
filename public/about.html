A Message from Our Founder

"Hello, I'm Saurabh Kumar. As someone who has experienced the struggles of competitive exam preparation, I understand the challenges every aspirant goes through."

I started NirnayPath with a simple vision: 'Har student ko mile barabar mauka'. I saw brilliant minds giving up because they couldn't afford expensive coaching. We built this platform to bring high-quality mock tests to every corner of India—completely free.

Our mission is to build confidence, to empower, and to ensure that no student's dream is limited by their financial situation. Every feature on NirnayPath is designed with empathy for our aspirants.

Success is not about having resources, but about making the most of what you have. Your determination is your greatest asset.
