The Heart Behind NirnayPath

"As an engineer and a fellow aspirant, I witnessed firsthand how talented students from humble backgrounds were forced to abandon their government exam dreams—not because of a lack of ability, but because they couldn't afford expensive test series that cost thousands of rupees. This wasn't just a gap in the market; it was a gap in social justice."

"I built NirnayPath with a single promise: every Indian student, whether from a metro city or a remote village, deserves access to world-class mock tests absolutely free. Our platform is not a business; it is a movement to level the playing field. We measure our success by your selection, not by our revenue."

Selection depends on preparation, and preparation should not depend on your wallet.

Our Core Beliefs
