Our Founding Story

The Heart Behind NirnayPath

As an engineer and a fellow aspirant, I witnessed firsthand how talented students from humble backgrounds were forced to abandon their government exam dreams — not because of lack of ability, but because they couldn't afford expensive test series costing thousands of rupees.

I built NirnayPath with a single promise: every Indian student deserves access to world-class mock tests absolutely free. Our platform is not just a product; it is a movement to level the playing field.

Selection depends on preparation, and preparation should not depend on your wallet.

Saurabh Kumar

Founder & Lead Engineer, NirnayPath

Our Journey

How NirnayPath Grew

2023 — Idea

The Spark

Frustrated by expensive test series, Saurabh Kumar starts coding the first version of NirnayPath — a free, full-length BPSC mock test engine.

2024 — Launch

Going Public

NirnayPath launches publicly with 500+ questions, real-time leaderboards, and detailed performance analytics. 5,000 aspirants join in the first month.

2025 — Expansion

Multi-Exam Platform

UPSC, SSC CGL, Railway, and Banking exam suites added. Question bank crosses 10,000 items. AI-powered performance intelligence goes live.

2026 — Now

50,000+ & Growing

50,000+ active aspirants. Daily mock tests, consistency heatmaps, national rankings, and adaptive question generation — all free, all live.
