From an Aspirant's Heart to India's Future

"As an engineer and a fellow aspirant, I witnessed firsthand how talented students from humble backgrounds were forced to abandon their government exam dreams—not because of a lack of ability, but because they couldn't afford expensive test series that cost thousands of rupees. I saw my friends, bright minds with the potential to serve the nation, struggle with outdated books and pay-walled content. This realization sparked the fire in me."

"I built NirnayPath in 2026 with a single promise: every Indian student, whether from a metro city or a remote village in Bihar, deserves access to world‑class mock tests absolutely free. We don't just provide questions; we provide an ecosystem that builds confidence. This platform is my contribution to an India where financial status never limits potential."

"Our journey has just begun. With 50,000+ aspirants already on board, we are proving that high-quality education doesn't need a high price tag. We are committed to transparency, excellence, and the success of every single student who uses NirnayPath."

Success is not about having resources, but about making the most of what you have. Your determination is your greatest asset, and we are here to sharpen it.

Our Core Beliefs

Our Mission

We exist to demolish the financial barriers that prevent brilliant minds from competing in India's toughest government exams. By providing completely free, high‑quality, bilingual mock tests that mirror the real exam experience, we empower every aspirant to walk into the examination hall with confidence, not worry about the cost of their preparation.

  • Free forever, zero hidden costs
  • Authentic exam patterns
  • Bilingual (Hindi/English)

Our Vision

We envision an India where the only thing that determines a candidate's success is their hard work and dedication—not their wallet. Our goal is to reach every district, every village, and every student who dares to dream of serving the nation through civil services, banking, railways, or any government sector.

  • Reaching the remotest villages
  • 100% Equal Opportunity
  • Success based on merit
