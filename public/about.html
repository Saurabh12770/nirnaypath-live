About NirnayPath –
India's Choice for Success

We are more than just a mock test platform. We are a movement dedicated to ensuring that every Indian student has the resources to succeed in their government exam journey, regardless of their financial status.

Saurabh Kumar

Founder & Visionary, NirnayPath

From an Aspirant's Heart to India's Future

"As an engineer and a fellow aspirant, I witnessed firsthand how talented students from humble backgrounds were forced to abandon their government exam dreams—not because of a lack of ability, but because they couldn't afford expensive test series that cost thousands of rupees. The coaching industry had turned mock tests into a luxury product, and I knew this was deeply unfair."

"I built NirnayPath with a single promise: every Indian student, whether from a metro city or a remote village, deserves access to world-class mock tests absolutely free. This platform is not a business for me—it is a mission to level the playing field and ensure that financial constraints never kill a dream again."

"We are here to provide the most realistic, high-quality, and bilingual practice environment. Our goal is to serve 1 Crore aspirants by 2030, ensuring that merit is the only currency that matters in India's competitive landscape. Together, we will build a stronger, smarter India."

"Success is not about having resources, but about making the most of what you have. Your determination is your greatest asset. We are here to fuel that determination."

Our Core Beliefs & Commitment

Our Mission

We exist to demolish the financial barriers that prevent brilliant minds from competing in India's toughest government exams. By providing completely free, high-quality, bilingual mock tests that mirror the real exam experience, we empower every aspirant to walk into the examination hall with confidence, not worry about the cost of their preparation.

  • Free forever, zero hidden costs
  • Authentic exam patterns & difficulty
  • Instant Analytics & Personalised Feedback

Our Vision

We envision an India where the only thing that determines a candidate's success is their hard work and dedication—not their wallet. Our goal is to reach every district, every village, and every student who dares to dream of serving the nation through civil services, banking, railways, or any government sector.

  • Reaching the remotest corners of India
  • 100% Equal Opportunity for every student
  • Creating a Meritocratic India

Values That Drive Us

Empathy First

Built by aspirants, for aspirants. We understand your struggle because we've lived it. Every feature is designed to reduce your preparation stress.

Radical Equity

100% free forever. We believe education is a right, not a privilege. No premium tiers that gatekeep your success.

Excellence

No compromises on quality. Every question is crafted by subject experts to match the rigorous standards of UPSC and State PSCs.

Innovation

Using technology to level the playing field. Our AI-driven analytics provide insights that were previously only available to elite coaching students.
