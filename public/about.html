The Heart Behind NirnayPath

"As an engineer and a fellow aspirant, I witnessed firsthand how talented students from humble backgrounds were forced to abandon their government exam dreams—not because of a lack of ability, but because they couldn't afford expensive test series that cost thousands of rupees."

"I built NirnayPath with a single promise: every Indian student deserves access to world-class mock tests absolutely free. Our platform is not a business; it is a movement to level the playing field."

Selection depends on preparation, and preparation should not depend on your wallet.
