Methodologies & Policies

Shift Normalization Methodology
NirnayPath utilizes the standard Equipercentile Equating method as recommended by leading psychometric bodies. This ensures that variations in paper difficulty across multiple shifts do not unfairly advantage or disadvantage any candidate.
Data Privacy & Security Commitments
All exam data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). PII data is masked during support and analytics reviews. Our architecture complies with national data localization regulations.
Appeal Timeline & Process
Candidates have a 7-day window post-answer key release to file disputes via the Support Portal. All appeals are reviewed by subject matter experts, and full fee refunds are initiated if a challenge is upheld.
